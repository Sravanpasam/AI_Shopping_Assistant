# Returns, Exchanges and Refunds Policy

## Return window
Most items can be returned within 14 days of delivery. The 14 days start on the day the order is marked as delivered.

## Condition of returned items
Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with all tags, accessories and manuals included. Returns without the original packaging may be refused or partially refunded.

## Items that cannot be returned
Opened beauty, personal care and hygiene products cannot be returned for safety reasons, unless they arrived damaged or defective. Gift cards, digital products and items marked as final sale are not returnable.

## Wrong size, wrong item or change of mind
For footwear and clothing, an unworn item can be exchanged for another size within 14 days. If the size you need is out of stock, you will receive a refund. For change of mind, the customer pays the return shipping fee of AED 15.

## Damaged, defective or incorrect items
If an item arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, report it within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We will arrange a free pickup and offer a replacement or a full refund, including the original shipping cost.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive and inspect the item. Cash on delivery orders are refunded to a bank account or store credit, as the customer chooses.

## How to start a return
Open your order in the account page and select Return Item, or contact customer support with your order number.
