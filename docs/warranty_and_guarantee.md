# Warranty and Guarantee Policy

## General rule
Warranty depends on the product category. The warranty period starts on the delivery date. Keep your order confirmation as proof of purchase.

## Warranty by category
- Electronics (headphones, phones, tablets, chargers, cameras): 12 months manufacturer warranty against defects.
- Large and small home appliances (kitchen appliances, vacuum cleaners, irons): 24 months manufacturer warranty against defects.
- Footwear and clothing: 3 months warranty against manufacturing defects such as sole separation or seam failure. Normal wear is not covered.
- Home and kitchen items without electronics (cookware, storage, decor): 6 months warranty against manufacturing defects.
- Beauty, personal care and grooming products: no warranty. Our 14-day return rules for damaged or defective items still apply.
- Gift cards: no warranty.

## What is covered
The warranty covers manufacturing defects and faults that appear during normal use.

## What is not covered
Accidental damage, water damage, misuse, unauthorized repairs, normal wear and tear, and cosmetic damage that does not affect how the product works.

## How to make a warranty claim
Contact customer support with your order number and a description or photo of the problem. We will either repair the item, replace it, or refund it if neither is possible. Claims are processed within 10 business days.

## Guarantee
All products sold in the store are genuine and sourced from authorized suppliers. If an item is found to be counterfeit, we will give a full refund.
