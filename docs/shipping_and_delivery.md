# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Where we deliver
We deliver across all seven emirates of the UAE. Delivery to other GCC countries (Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman) is available for most items. We do not deliver outside the GCC.

## Delivery times
- Dubai, Sharjah and Ajman: standard delivery in 1 to 2 business days.
- Abu Dhabi and other emirates: standard delivery in 2 to 4 business days.
- Other GCC countries: 5 to 10 business days.
- Express delivery, available in Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi: next day for orders placed before 2 PM.

## Shipping fees
- Standard delivery is free for orders of AED 150 or more. Below AED 150 the fee is AED 10.
- Express delivery costs AED 25.
- GCC delivery costs AED 40 for the first kilogram and AED 10 for each extra kilogram.

## Delivery attempts
The courier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. If all attempts fail, the order is returned to us and refunded after deducting the shipping fee.

## Delays
Delivery times are estimates. During sales periods and public holidays, delivery may take 1 to 3 extra days. You will receive a message if your order is delayed.

## Delivery to a different address
The delivery address can be changed before the order is shipped. After it has shipped, contact the courier using the tracking number.
