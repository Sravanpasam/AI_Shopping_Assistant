# Order Tracking and Cancellation

## Order statuses
- Processing: we have received your order and are preparing it.
- Shipped: your order has left our warehouse. You can track it with the tracking number.
- Out for delivery: the courier will deliver it today.
- Delivered: your order has been delivered.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled and any payment is being refunded.
- Returned: the order was returned and the refund is being processed.

## How to track an order
Ask the assistant with your order number, for example ORD-10025, or use the tracking number in your shipping email.

## Cancelling an order
An order can be cancelled for free while its status is Processing. Once it is Shipped it can no longer be cancelled, but you can refuse the delivery or return it after it arrives under our return policy.

## Changing an order
Quantity, size and delivery address can be changed while the order is Processing. Contact customer support with your order number.

## Refund time after cancellation
Cancelled prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.

## Speaking to a person
If the assistant cannot solve your problem, or you are not happy with an answer, ask for a human agent and your conversation will be passed to customer support.
