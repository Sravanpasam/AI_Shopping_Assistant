# Payment Methods and Cash on Delivery

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, bank transfer, and cash on delivery.

## Cash on delivery (COD)
Cash on delivery is available for orders up to AED 2,000 within the UAE. A COD fee of AED 10 is added to the order. Please keep the exact amount ready, as couriers may not carry change.

## When COD is not available
COD is not available for gift cards, for orders above AED 2,000, for orders delivered outside the UAE, or for orders that include pre-order items.

## Installments
Orders above AED 500 can be paid in 3 or 6 monthly installments with selected credit cards.

## Taxes
Prices include 5% VAT. The VAT amount is shown on your invoice.

## Failed or declined payments
If a card payment is declined, the order is not placed and no money is taken. If money is taken but the order does not show in your account, it is automatically refunded within 3 to 5 business days.

## Invoices
An invoice is sent by email when your order ships. You can also download it from your order page.
